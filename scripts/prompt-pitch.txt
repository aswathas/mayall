## docs/pitch/index.html — THE DECK
A self-contained slide deck. ONE html file, no build step, no external
dependencies (no CDN, no webfonts — use system stacks with a mono fallback).
It must open by double-click and work offline.

Requirements:
- 8 slides, sized 1280x720, scaled to fit any viewport (CSS transform scale on
  a fixed-size stage).
- Keyboard navigation: arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown, Space. Plus click zones and
  a visible slide counter and progress rail.
- Print to PDF must work: `@media print` lays each slide out one per page, in
  the right colours (use `print-color-adjust: exact`).
- The Red Room palette: ground #090607, panels #110b0d, hairlines #2b1a1e,
  ink #f7eded, ink-dim #c3a7aa, arterial #ff3b30, ember #ff7a45, amber #f5b942,
  mint #46d39a, bone #efe6e4. Mono for all data, a grotesque for headings.
- Slide plan:
  1. TITLE — "Mayall", one line on what it is, and the one thing it does that
     explorers cannot: show the call-by-call reconstruction of an exploit.
  2. THE PROBLEM — explorers show you that; incident responders need how.
     Use the six vectors as a typographic list, not six cards.
  3. THE DEMO — what the audience will see in the next 90 seconds, with the
     click path (http://localhost:3000/lab → Run → watch the trace).
  4. THE EVIDENCE — the reentrancy run: 83-node call tree, `withdraw()` invoked
     7 times nested, 6 ETH drained, storage diff showing `totalWithdrawn` 0 → 6
     ETH while the balance falls. This is the money slide — make the nested
     repetition the visual.
  5. THE DETECTORS — 10 pattern detectors, confidence + evidence, the two
     weakest honest limitations (some detectors key off narrative keywords).
  6. THE ARTEFACT — what comes out: the forensic bundle ZIP and the
     chain-of-custody manifest. One spec-sheet block, not a grid of icons.
  7. HONESTY SLIDE — what is real and what is simulated. State it plainly.
  8. THE ASK / WHAT'S NEXT — what we would do with more time (mainnet fork
     replay, more chains, ML-assisted triage).
- Motion: one authored moment per slide transition (a subtle arterial wipe),
  CSS-only, and fully disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- No emoji as icons. Use inline SVG or drawn shapes only.
- No lorem ipsum, no placeholder copy — every word is real.
